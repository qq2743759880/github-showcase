# Bounded fact discovery

Read the project root listing, manifests, public entry points, examples, tests and relevant docs. Stay inside adapter roots; exclude real configuration, environment files, credentials and private parallel projects. Do not scan secrets in their original private location.

Write a ledger with claim, source-relative file and line, status (verified / user-confirmed / uncertain / missing), public approval and contradiction. Inspect enough source to verify every public API, relationship and Quick Start command. Bound the search by the requested showcase; do not generate a whole onboarding portal.

## Decide whether discovery is sufficient

After the bounded pass, check the claims needed to explain the project, its public interface, architecture and Quick Start. Internal discovery is sufficient when each consequential claim has current cited evidence, or can be omitted/qualified without making the remaining narrative or instructions misleading. Simple direct call paths, manifests and examples ordinarily suffice. Repository size alone does not establish complexity.

Record a short decision in the run ledger: required question, inspected evidence, remaining gap, choice/reason, approved roots/exclusions/budget, and extension resource status. Use these choices in this order:

1. **STOP/BLOCKED**: the next useful step crosses a private/excluded root, requires unapproved execution/network/history, or the missing prerequisite prevents the requested action. Preserve the gap; escalation grants no new authority. Missing LICENSE remains a distribution blocker.
2. **INTERNAL_SUFFICIENT**: necessary claims are supported. Leave optional absent metrics, media, future design or inaccessible external behavior unknown/omitted. Do not invoke a Skill to fill an absence.
3. **ESCALATE_CKB**: a consequential question remains unresolved after bounded recon, there is a useful evidence trail inside approved scope, and resolving it requires deeper study of indirect registrations/composition, cross-process or async state ownership, configuration precedence, failure/edge paths, or a material docs/code/test contradiction. Follow [the full Optional extension contract](fact-escalation.md). Read that workflow and resolve its original resources only at this decision.
4. **UNRESOLVED**: deeper study has no available evidence trail, or its bounded budget is exhausted. Qualify/omit the claim if possible; block dependent instructions or architecture claims otherwise. Never turn an unknown into a confident public fact.

An evidenced queue or monorepo can still be INTERNAL_SUFFICIENT; a small dynamically composed application can require ESCALATE_CKB. Base the decision on the unresolved question and user impact, not a numerical complexity score. An explicit request for full codebase research also selects the complete Optional workflow inside its authorized scope.

## Return to composition

Compose the approved narrative only from public rows. Separate design intent from executable behavior. Record absent metrics and demo. Hash cited public sources to detect stale facts. Proceed to editing after unknowns and limitations are visible. After escalation, merge only supported findings into the fact ledger; keep the bounded knowledge artifact as provenance.
